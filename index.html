The Lore

Exiled From
The North Pole.

Kris Kringle IV asked his father one question: why does Christmas only happen once a year?

For that, he was cast out. Exiled from the North Pole with nothing but the fastest sleigh, his Elves, and a mother's handmade music box.

He landed in Orlando and spent a decade building a mythology in exile — a rock-and-roll Christmas rebellion that proved him right. From Frosty to Bowie, Dickens to Queen, the band built the ultimate sonic sleigh ride.

Now they're bringing the rebellion to Edinburgh.

The Roster

Meet The Elves.

Centuries old. Stripped of various honors. The actual people who taught your favorite rock stars how to do what they do. Now flying to Scotland.
