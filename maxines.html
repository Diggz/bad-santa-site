Exiled From the North Poleat Maxine's on Shine

Before Edinburgh, the greatest holiday band of all time previews their Mythic Immersive Cabaret Rock Opera.

The Show.

The show tells the story of Kris Kringle IV, thrown out by his immortal father for asking why Christmas only happens once a year — and the century of unanswered letters that proved him right. He takes the fastest sleigh, his Elves, and a mother's handmade music box, building a ten-year mythology in exile. From Frosty to Bowie, Dickens to Queen, the band summons you onto the ultimate sonic sleigh ride, gets your stocking stuffed, and reminds you what you used to believe.
