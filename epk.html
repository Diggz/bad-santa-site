The Band

Bad Santa & The Angry Elves.

Bad Santa and The Angry Elves aren't just the greatest Christmas Band of all time, they're the coolest cats in rock history, belting out hits with the swagger of seasoned rock gods. Their live shows hit harder than a frozen snowball fight, with lyrics sharper than a reindeer's antlers and guitar riffs hotter than chestnuts roasting on an open fire.


After being permanently banned from the North Pole, they've set their sights on world domination, spreading Christmas cheer all year long. Imagine a tour that fuses the legendary stage presence of Bowie, the electrifying energy of Van Halen, the unapologetic style of Prince, and the rebellious spirit of The Ramones — creating a spectacle for the naughty and the nice.
