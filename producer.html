The Vision

Beyond Edinburgh.

For over a decade, Bad Santa and the Angry Elves have been living in the presents — selling out venues across Orlando, building a fiercely loyal community, and proving that a ridiculous idea can become something real if you believe in it hard enough.

Now we've been officially invited to perform at the Edinburgh Fringe Festival — 25 nights in a stone cave under the city, the biggest arts festival in the world. But Edinburgh isn't the finish line. It's the launchpad.

We're building toward a full theatrical expansion — a proper production with a narrative arc, original compositions, and the kind of show that could tour internationally. Executive Producers help fund the Fringe run, the media development, and the long-term growth of this thing from a beloved local institution into something the world gets to experience.

This is the ground floor. The people who come in now become part of the origin story.
